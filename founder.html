Aiman El-Ramly

Aiman El-Ramly brings more than three decades of enterprise leadership to ZECO, with experience building and growing businesses across technology, engineering, artificial intelligence, SaaS, and professional services.

As a founder, executive, board director, and speaker, Aiman understands the priorities that bring business audiences together.

Business experience behind purposeful events

Aiman’s experience provides ZECO with a business perspective on event planning, from understanding what an audience needs to shaping discussions around relevant industry challenges.

  • 30+ years of enterprise leadership across technology and engineering.

  • Founder and CEO of AEPG, supporting business growth and platform development.

  • Certified Management Consultant advising organizations on strategy.

  • Industry contributor on AI, energy markets, information strategy, and infrastructure.
